# Light minimal system

## Color

- Canvas: `#FFFFFF`, `#FAFAF8`, or another near-white with high luminance.
- Surface: white or a subtle tint no darker than a light neutral.
- Text: near-black such as `#171717`.
- Muted text: medium gray such as `#666666`.
- Accent: one saturated brand color used for the category, small details, and CTA.
- Border: one quiet neutral such as `#E7E7EA`.

Never use a dark canvas, black card, inverted dark variant, gradient background, neon color wash, or low-contrast pastel text.

## Typography

- Use one sans-serif family with regular, semibold, and bold weights.
- Use sentence case for headlines.
- Reserve uppercase for a short category label.
- Prefer size and whitespace over decorative type treatments.
- Avoid outlined, condensed novelty, handwritten, or display fonts for news copy.

## Shape and depth

- Use one corner-radius family: 24–32 px for large cards, 16–24 px for pills.
- Prefer thin borders to shadows.
- If a shadow is essential, use one soft low-opacity shadow with no colored glow.
- Do not stack multiple nested cards.

## Density

- Keep at least 64 px of outer whitespace on a 1080 px canvas.
- Use no more than six visible regions: header, category, visual, headline, summary, footer.
- Remove any element that does not improve identity, comprehension, attribution, or action.
- Split dense content into another slide instead of shrinking the type.

## Imagery

- Use one decisive image with `cover` cropping.
- Keep faces, logos, and essential UI inside the safe crop.
- Never stretch, over-sharpen, or place a heavy color overlay over documentary imagery.
- Use a visible placeholder only during drafting.
